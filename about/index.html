About Full Forms

Full Forms explains the short forms, abbreviations and acronyms that people in India meet at school, at work, at the bank, in the news and in messages. It has 481 entries in 11 subjects. Every entry gives the full form and a line on what the thing is, and the 31 most looked-up ones have a page of their own.

How the entries were written

Every entry and explanation is our own writing. We include only expansions that are settled and widely documented: the names of organisations as they themselves write them, the standard names of degrees, technical terms as used in textbooks, and dictionary etymologies for Latin short forms. Where a popular "full form" is not real, as with Wi-Fi, SOS, NEWS or POLICE, the site says so.

What the site does not do

It does not give fees, dates, eligibility rules, rates or limits, because those change. For anything you must act on, go to the official body named in the entry. Medical and legal entries explain a term; they are not advice. This site is independent and is not connected with any organisation, board, company or examination named on it.

Mistakes

If an expansion is wrong, or an organisation has changed its name, please tell us through the contact page and we will correct it.
