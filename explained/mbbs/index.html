MBBS full form: Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery

How to say it: letter by letter.

MBBS stands for Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery. It is the basic professional degree that a person must earn to become a doctor of modern medicine in India and in many other countries that follow the British tradition.

Why the letters do not match the English words

The abbreviation comes from Latin: Medicinae Baccalaureus, Baccalaureus Chirurgiae. MB is the bachelor of medicine and BS the bachelor of surgery (some universities write ChB, from chirurgiae). Although the name lists two degrees, it is awarded as one.

The course

In India the course runs for five and a half years: four and a half years of study followed by a compulsory rotating internship of one year in a hospital. Admission is through the national entrance examination, NEET (UG). The course and the colleges are regulated by the National Medical Commission.

After MBBS

A graduate registers with the medical council and may practise as a doctor. Many go on to postgraduate study:

  • MD: Doctor of Medicine, in subjects such as general medicine or paediatrics.
  • MS: Master of Surgery, in surgical subjects.
  • DNB: Diplomate of National Board.
  • DM and MCh: super-speciality degrees taken after MD or MS.

Other first degrees in health

  • BDS: Bachelor of Dental Surgery.
  • BAMS: Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery.
  • BHMS: Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery.
  • BUMS: Bachelor of Unani Medicine and Surgery.
  • BSc Nursing and BPharm, for nursing and pharmacy.

How to write it

Write MBBS in capitals without full stops, after the doctor's name: Dr Asha Verma, MBBS.
